About Me

A student who enjoys solving real problems

I am an Information Technology student at Kirinyaga University, Kenya, currently working towards a Bachelor’s Degree in Information Technology. Through my studies I am developing practical knowledge and skills in IT support, computer hardware, software troubleshooting, networking, databases and web development.

I enjoy learning new technologies, solving problems, working hands-on with computers, and applying what I learn in class to practical situations. Whether it is installing software, tracing a network connection problem, organising data in a database or building a simple web page, I like seeing the theory turn into something that actually works.

I am looking for opportunities to keep growing, to learn from experienced professionals, and to contribute to technology-driven organisations.

Career Objective

To grow into a competent and dependable IT professional by building strong practical skills in IT support, networking, databases and web development, while contributing positively to technology-driven organisations and continuing to learn throughout my career.

What I Bring Along

  • Willingness to learn — I ask questions, take notes and practise until I understand.
  • Problem-solving — I break a big issue into small, testable steps.
  • Teamwork — I share information clearly and support my group members.
  • Adaptability — I am comfortable with new tools and changing tasks.
  • Responsibility — I finish what I start and respect deadlines.
  • Attention to detail — I check my work carefully before calling it done.
